Lesson 7 · 18 min

HR for people and agents

When someone joins Horizonte, they get a file, a badge, system access and a person responsible for them. When they leave, access is cut the same day. Hora joined with none of that: in the inventory, the owner is IT. And the automatic summary was suspended, but nobody said who reads the conversations now. In this lesson you handle agents joining and leaving the way you handle people.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1What an agent's onboarding needs to include
  2. 2The agent register, like the list of who works at the company
  3. 3Train the people who supervise, not just the agent
  4. 4Offboard an agent without leaving orphaned work or open access

Goal: organize an agent's onboarding, follow-up and offboarding the way you do for people: owner, access, record and who takes over the work.

02 · HR for people and agents in practice

Four steps, with Hora and Horizonte's automatic summary.

1. An agent is onboarded too

Deloitte (Tech Trends 2026) treats agents as a workforce that needs onboarding in the data and the operation, graduated autonomy with human-oversight triggers, and notes that the human supervisors also need training.

In practice, onboarding comes down to three questions: who owns the result, what it can access and with which identity, and who supervises it. Without a business owner, the agent stays with IT, which is not accountable for the business result.

At Horizonte (fictional study company)In the lab inventory, the person responsible for Hora is Rafael, from IT. In the retroactive onboarding, the owner becomes Patrícia; Rafael keeps looking after the technical access.

2. The agent register

Every company knows who works there. The agent register is the same list for agents: name, role, owner, who supervises, version, access, start date, next review and status (active, on trial, suspended, offboarded).

Start with IT's inventory and add what the shadow AI survey found. An agent that is not in the register has no owner, and an agent with no owner has no one to switch it off.

At HorizonteThe register starts with Hora and the email assistant, which are in the official inventory, and with the daily service summary (F10), which the inventory didn't even list. Then come the tools the survey found on its own (F12).

3. Train the people who supervise

Onboarding an agent creates new work for someone: reviewing a sample, reading an exception package, deciding with the right verb, pressing the off switch. That person needs to be trained, with time set aside for it.

Write down who supervises each agent, what that person has learned and who covers for them on vacation. A supervisor with no training becomes a rubber stamp.

At HorizonteJuliana supervises the queue with Patrícia. Training: the review checklist from lesson 4, the exception package and the off switch, tested once with Rafael. Backup: Sandra.

4. Offboarding is a process

Shutting down an agent takes the same care as an offboarding: cut access the same day, say who takes over the work and from when, decide what to do with the data left with the vendor, and keep the record.

The most forgotten step is the work the agent was doing. If no one takes it over, it quietly lands on people who were already overloaded, or it disappears without anyone noticing.

At HorizonteThe daily summary (F10) was suspended until the vendor is replaced. Until then, Juliana reads only the exception queue and a sample of 20 conversations a day. Rafael cuts off the sending and asks the service how to delete what was already sent.

Prompt · look for gaps
You are reviewing the onboarding or offboarding of an AI agent at a
company. Do not decide anything: point out gaps.

1. Is there a business owner with a name (not just IT)?
2. Is any access bigger than needed, or does it use a person's
   credentials?
3. Has the supervisor been trained, and do they have a backup?
4. In offboarding: who takes over the work, starting when, and was
   that person consulted?
5. What happens to the data left with the vendor?

Simulation: illustrative example

Retroactive onboarding · Hora · 11/05 · Horizonte, fictional study company

  • Owner: Patrícia (before: Rafael, in the inventory) · technical lead: Rafael.
  • Supervisor: Juliana, trained in the review checklist, the exception package and the off switch · backup: Sandra.
  • Access: catalog (read) · customer record from the conversation (read) · orders app (write up to R$ 5,000 and 5%) · its own identity.
  • Trial period: sample of 20 conversations per week until 12/04.

Offboarding (suspension) · daily customer service summary · F10

  • Reason: conversations with tax ID and address in a free service outside Brazil, with no contract.
  • Stopped: the 6 p.m. automation, by Rafael, checked by Patrícia.
  • Who takes over: Juliana reads the exception queue and 20 conversations a day, agreed with her; anything that doesn't fit goes up to Patrícia.
  • Data with the vendor: Rafael requests deletion and records the answer.
  • Register: status = suspended until the vendor is replaced.
  1. IT as owner. IT takes care of access; the result belongs to whoever answers for the process.
  2. Borrowed password. An agent using a person's account has no record of its own and can't be shut down without shutting down the person.
  3. Untrained supervisor. It becomes a rubber stamp.
  4. Orphaned work. The agent was shut down and no one took over: the work comes back in secret or disappears.
  5. Forgetting the vendor. Data left there stays there until someone asks for it to be deleted.

04 · Practice

Onboard Hora and turn off the automatic summary; then build the agent register for your area.

1 · Onboard Hora, turn off the summary

30 min

Do the retroactive onboarding of Hora using the template. Then do the offboarding (suspension) of the daily customer service summary, which sends conversations to a free service with no contract. Build Horizonte's agent register from what the inventory and the survey show. Run the prompt on both documents.

Done when: the onboarding has a business owner, a trained supervisor with a backup, and access with limits; the offboarding says who takes over reading the conversations, starting when, and what happens to the data with the vendor; and the register has at least 3 agents or automations, each with an owner and a status.

2 · The register for your area

40 min

List the AI agents and automations in your area, including the ones your team uses on their own. Build the register. Pick one of them and do the full onboarding (or the offboarding, if that applies).

Done when: the register has every agent you know of, each with an owner and a status, there is at least one complete checklist, and every named supervisor has a backup.

Lab materials: IT inventory and customer service summary (F10).
