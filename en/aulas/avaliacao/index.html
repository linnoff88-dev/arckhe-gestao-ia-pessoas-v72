Lesson 6 · 18 min

Evaluating people who work with agents

Juliana's goal is still "conversations handled per day." But Hora handles most of them. Measuring Juliana by volume is measuring Hora, and it also pushes Juliana to take back the easy conversations. Her work has changed to deciding, reviewing, improving and taking care of people. In this lesson, the evaluation changes along with it.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Why the volume goal ended up measuring the agent
  2. 2The four criteria: deciding, reviewing, improving, relationship and teaching
  3. 3Where the evidence for each criterion comes from
  4. 4What can't go into the evaluation

Goal: change the evaluation of a role from "how much it did" to "what it decided, reviewed, improved and taught," with evidence for each criterion.

02 · How to change the evaluation

Four steps, with Juliana's sheet as an example.

1. Volume changed owners

When the agent does most of the volume, the person's volume target measures the agent. Worse: it pushes the person to take the easy conversations back, or to leave the exception queue waiting.

Take volume out of the individual evaluation and keep it on the team dashboard. The evaluation question becomes what the person did with the work only they can do.

At Horizonte (fictional study company)Juliana's old target: 120 conversations a day. With Hora, she handles the ones left over, harder and longer. The old target would fail her for the most important work.

2. Four criteria

Decide (exceptions on time, right verb, reason), review (errors found and errors that slipped through), improve (what became a rule or test), relationships and teaching (difficult customer, colleague trained).

Set weights by role. For people who serve customers, deciding and reviewing weigh more; for those who train the junior, teaching weighs more.

On Juliana's sheetDecide 35% · Review 25% · Improve 20% · Relationships and teaching 20%.

3. Evidence, not impression

For each criterion, write where the evidence comes from: the queue log, the review spreadsheet, the change requests, the cases told. An evaluation without a source becomes sympathy.

Describe each score on the 1-to-4 scale with a behavior. "3 = resolves on time with a reason, sometimes with the wrong verb" can be evaluated; "3 = good" cannot.

Evidence for "decide"Queue log: 26 of 28 exceptions this month on time, all with a reason, 2 with the wrong verb (sent back what should have been stopped). Fictional numbers.

4. What stays out

Never punish someone who points out an agent error: a found error is a contribution. Never use an agent's number as a personal target. Never compare a person's speed with the agent's.

In the evaluation conversation, the person brings three cases from the period: a hard decision, an error they caught and an improvement. The conversation starts from them, not from the spreadsheet.

At HorizonteJuliana brought the hotel request she held back, the deadline error from Hora that she logged and the test case she proposed. The conversation lasted 30 minutes and ended with a teaching goal: help Lucas in week 3.

04 · Practice

Redo Juliana's sheet; then one for a role on your team, with the person.

1 · Juliana's sheet

25 min

Using the matrix from lesson 2 and what Juliana now does, write her evaluation sheet with the four criteria, weights, evidence and scale. Run the wrong-incentive prompt and fix it.

Done when: the sheet has 4 criteria with weights adding up to 100%, evidence with a source for each, a 1-to-4 scale described by behavior in at least 2 criteria, "conversations per day" left out of the individual evaluation and mistakes found counted in her favor.

2 · A sheet for a role on your team

40 min

Choose a role on your team that already works with an agent, or will. Write the sheet and show it to the person before the next period starts. Note what they would change.

Done when: the sheet has the 4 criteria with evidence and scale, no criterion measures the agent's volume, the person read it before the period and at least one adjustment came from them.

06 · Summary

Four criteriaDecide, review, improve, relationships and teaching.
