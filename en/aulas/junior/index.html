Lesson 4 · 18 min

The junior who supervises agents

Lucas joins in November as a sales assistant. Before, he would have learned by answering catalog questions and entering simple orders. Today that is Hora's work. If he starts out only reviewing what Hora does, he will review without knowing what to look for. In this lesson you redesign the first 30 days of a new hire, without skipping the learning.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Why the first step of the career ladder disappeared
  2. 2Do it by hand before reviewing the agent
  3. 3Teach reviewing with a list of what to look for
  4. 4Give responsibility one step at a time, with a clear criterion

Goal: redesign the first 30 days of a new hire: what they do by hand, what they review, who they learn with, and when they get more responsibility.

01 · The first 30 days

Four steps, one per week. Each one adds a verb. Click each step.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Path recommended by INEMA: do before reviewing, one new verb per week. The sample and hit-rate numbers at Horizonte are didactic, fictional thresholds; adjust them to your risk.

02 · How to redesign the entry path

The reason for each step and what Horizonte did with Lucas.

1. The step disappeared

The tasks where the junior used to learn (the simple question, the routine order) are the first ones to go to the agent. In the INEMA overview of Oct/2026, the share of entry-level openings fell from 29% to 10% (Indeed).

Critics of flattened structures remind us that removing a layer does not eliminate its work, and training juniors is part of that work. If no one puts the step back, the company loses the people who will supervise the agents tomorrow.

At Horizonte (fictional study company)Lucas would have learned the catalog by answering customers. Today Hora answers. If no one plans for it, he spends the first month watching the queue without understanding what he is seeing.

2. By hand first

Someone who has never done it doesn't know what to look for when reviewing. In the first week, set aside a few cases for the junior to do by hand, next to someone experienced, with no production pressure.

It is not a waste: it is the cost of training the eye that will check the agent. Choose varied cases, including a hard one.

At HorizonteWeek 1: Lucas handles 10 orders by hand with Sandra, a salesperson for 12 years, reads the discount policy and sits in on two of Juliana's calls with an angry customer.

3. Review that teaches

Reviewing is a skill, not a punishment for the newcomer. Give a short list of what to look for and have the person review the same sample that an experienced colleague reviewed.

Compare the markings. The disagreement is the lesson: why the mentor saw a problem the junior did not see, or the other way around.

At HorizonteWeek 2: 20 Hora conversations per week; Lucas marks them, Sandra marks the same ones, and the two lists are compared on Friday.

4. One verb per week

Each week adds one responsibility: do, review, send back, take care of a slice. To move up a step, use a criterion you can check, agreed on beforehand.

A 20-minute conversation each week closes the step: what they learned, what they got wrong, whether they pass or repeat. Repeating the week is not failing; it is the person's own pace.

Week 2 criterion (teaching example)Agree with Sandra on at least 18 of the 20 conversations and explain the disagreements. Below that, one more week of paired review.

Simulation: illustrative example

30-day path · Lucas, 22 years old · sales assistant · start 03/11 · mentor: Sandra · manager: Patrícia · conversation: Friday, 20 min · Horizonte, fictional study company

  1. Week 1 · Do: 10 orders by hand with Sandra; reads the discount policy and the table; sits in with Juliana on 2 angry customers. Passes when he explains the policy with its three tiers (5%, 10%, above) and enters the 10 orders with no price errors.
  2. Week 2 · Review: 20 Hora conversations with the list; Sandra reviews the same ones. Passes with 18 of 20 markings matching and the disagreements explained.
  3. Week 3 · Send back: prepares the send-backs for orders missing a CNPJ or address; Juliana checks before they go out. Approving a discount stays with Patrícia. Passes with 5 send-backs with no content corrections.
  4. Week 4 · Take care: the weekly sample of catalog questions; proposes 1 new test case for Hora.

After 30 days: joins the exception queue with Juliana; review on 04/12.

  1. Starting with review. Without having done it by hand, the person marks "ok" on what they do not understand.
  2. Reviewing alone. Without a partner, nobody knows whether the review is any good.
  3. A "when they are ready" criterion. Without a number or a deliverable, passing becomes a feeling.
  4. Giving someone else's authority limit. A junior who approves the manager's discount learns the shortcut, not the rule.
  5. A mentor with no time. Agree on the mentor's hours with them and with management, or the path stays on paper.

04 · Practice

Design Lucas's path; then the path for the person joining your team.

1 · Lucas's path

25 min

Build Lucas's 30-day path with the template, using Horizonte's fictional study policy. For week 2, run lab conversations c-201 to c-203 through the list of what to look for yourself, as if you were the mentor. Run the review prompt.

Done when: the path has all 4 weeks, each with at least 1 task done by hand or in a pair, a passing criterion with a number, the mentor by name, no decision within Patrícia's authority limit in Lucas's hands, and the 3 lab conversations reviewed with the list.

2 · The path for the person joining your team

40 min

Choose who is about to join or who joined less than 6 months ago. Build the path, agree on the hours with the mentor, and schedule the first weekly conversation.

Done when: the path is written with 4 weeks and criteria, the mentor accepted the hours in writing, the list of what to look for is adapted to your agent, and the first conversation is on the calendar.

Lab piece: WhatsApp agent (conversations c-201 to c-203), a good sample for week 2.
