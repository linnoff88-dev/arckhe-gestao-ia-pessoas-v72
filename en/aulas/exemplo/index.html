Lesson 1 · 15 min

The manager sets the example

Patrícia's team already uses AI: one salesperson pastes the customer spreadsheet into a personal chatbot, another records visits without telling anyone. Nobody said anything because nobody asked, and the manager wasn't using it. Before any rule, the team needs to see their leader using agents for real, making mistakes and fixing them, and to know what is and isn't allowed.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Why the manager's example comes before the rule
  2. 2Show the agent's mistakes, not just its wins
  3. 3Agree on the data rule in three lines
  4. 4Make time and a channel for the team to experiment

Goal: use agents in your own work in front of the team and open a shared space to experiment, with a rule for customer data.

02 · Four steps to get started

Each tab gives the reason and what Patrícia did at Horizonte.

1. Start at your own desk

You learn to manage agents by using them, the same way nobody learned to lead just by reading a leadership book. Pick tasks you do every week, that the team knows, and where a mistake doesn't reach the customer without you seeing it.

There is also a market data point. Microsoft, Work Trend Index 2026 (05/2026): when the manager uses AI in front of the team, perceived value goes up 17 points. This is research from a company that sells the tool; read it as a signal, not as proof.

At Horizonte (fictional study company)Patrícia chose: the weekly sales summary, a draft reply to a customer who complained about a delay, and Monday's meeting agenda. None of them goes out without her reading it.

2. Show the mistake

Showing only the polished result teaches the team to trust without checking. Show the agent's draft, the part that was wrong, and what you changed.

Say out loud what you don't know yet. The team learns that the work now includes reviewing, and that whoever points out the agent's mistake is helping, not getting in the way.

At Monday's meeting"The summary invented a customer who didn't buy this week. I caught it because I checked it against the orders app. That's why nobody sends a summary to Carlos without checking it."

3. Set the data rule

When the leader starts using it, the team gets excited and brings out what they already use in secret. That's the time to agree on what is and isn't allowed, without punishing anyone who speaks up.

The rule fits in three lines: what's allowed, what's never done, and who to ask. Don't ban AI: ban the path that exposes the customer and offer a safe path.

At HorizonteThe internal survey found the customer spreadsheet in a personal chatbot and visits recorded without notice (lab failure F12). The rule: customer data only in company tools; record only with notice; if in doubt, ask Patrícia or Rafael.

4. Make room to experiment

Experimenting without time becomes experimenting in secret, on a phone, after hours. Reserve one hour a week on the team's calendar and a channel to show what worked and what didn't.

The most important agreement is that no one is punished for telling what they use. Without that, the next survey comes back empty and the risk is still there.

At HorizonteThursday, 4 to 5 p.m., everyone tests one of their own tasks. Friday, 10 minutes in the meeting: one win and one mistake from each person. The channel is a team group with the rule pinned at the top.

Simulation: illustrative example

Week's agreement · sales · 10/26 · leader: Patrícia · Horizonte, fictional study company

  1. Tasks: sales week summary (Monday) · draft reply to the customer who complained about a delay (Tuesday) · meeting agenda (Wednesday). Tool: AI assistant on a company account, under contract. Shown at the Friday meeting, 9 a.m.
  2. Data rule: ALLOWED text without customer names, catalog and tables. NEVER a customer spreadsheet on a personal account, recording a visit without telling the customer. IN DOUBT Patrícia or Rafael.
  3. Space: Thursday, 4 to 5 p.m. · channel: group "sales · AI", rule pinned · no one is punished for telling what they use.
  4. Friday: one win and one mistake from each person.

Reactions: "can I use it for a proposal?" (yes, without the whole spreadsheet); "what about the 6 p.m. summary?" (stays with Juliana and Rafael, lesson 7).

  1. Rehearsed demo. If the task isn't really yours, the team can tell. Use the week's actual work.
  2. Only the win. If you don't show what you fixed, you teach people to trust without checking.
  3. A rule that bans everything. "Don't use AI" pushes the use onto personal phones. Ban the dangerous path and offer the safe one.
  4. Space without a date. "When we can" never happens. Day, time and channel.
  5. Punishing whoever spoke up. One scolding and the next survey comes back empty.

04 · Practice

Practice with Patrícia's team; then do your own real week.

1 · Patrícia's agreement

20 min

Read the 22 answers from the internal survey in the lab inventory. Write Patrícia's one-week agreement using the template: her three tasks, the data rule that resolves the sales team's two risky uses, and the space to experiment. Run the review prompt and fix what it points out.

Done when: the agreement has 3 tasks with a day and a tool, a data rule with at least 2 "nevers" taken from the survey (spreadsheet on a personal account and recording without notice), a day and time to experiment, and a named channel.

2 · Your week

1 week

Write your own agreement and follow it: three of your own tasks with an agent over the week, shown to the team at a meeting along with what you corrected. Note what the team asked or told you they already use.

Done when: the 3 tasks were done and shown at a meeting with a date, the data rule is pinned in the team channel, and you noted at least 2 reactions or questions from the team.
