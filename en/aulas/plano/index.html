Lesson 8 · 20 min

Project: the people plan + What changed

The owner wants to know if the team can handle the change; the team wants to know what happens to them. Both answers fit on one page: who does what, how the conversation was held, how new people are trained, which values apply to people and agents, how performance is evaluated, and how agents come in and go out. The project is this plan, approved by rubric.

02 · From draft to signature

Four steps. The Horizonte plan is practice; your area's plan is the one that counts.

1. One page

The page summarizes; the pieces prove it. Each block has two or three lines and points to the lesson document that backs it: matrix, script, path, values, sheets, register.

Mark what has already happened and what is only intent. "Meeting held on 10/30" and "meeting planned" carry different weight at signing.

At Horizonte (fictional study company)"People plan · sales · v1 · Nov 2026": one page up front; behind it, the pieces from lessons 1 to 7.

2. Run it through the rubric

The rubric adds up to 100 points across six blocks. You pass with 75 or more and all five must-haves checked. One missing must-have fails the plan, even at 95 points.

The must-haves protect people: a decision with a name on it, no promise the company hasn't made, learning by hand before reviewing, no one punished for pointing out an agent's mistake, no agent without an owner.

Panel testRun the panel prompt (next to this). It points out gaps; the score is up to you, and to whoever signs.

3. Those affected read first

In the people plan, the ones who read before signing are the people affected: they say whether the conversation, the path and the sheet happened as written. It's not a vote; it's a check.

Whoever signs is the person who answers for the people and the budget. They read the page, check the must-haves, and ask, ideally, about what is still unknown.

At HorizontePatrícia puts it together. Juliana checks her own conversation and her sheet; Sandra checks Lucas's path. Carlos reads it, asks what happens to customer service in a year, and signs knowing the honest answer is "we don't know yet."

4. Review date

The plan goes out with a review date: every quarter, or sooner if a new agent comes in, someone new joins, or the conversation with the team needs to be redone.

At the review, what was an intention becomes a fact or goes back into the plan: was the path completed? was the new sheet used? were the open questions answered?

RuleAn agent came in or left, or the work-split matrix changed: redo the conversation with those affected before touching the evaluation.

04 · What changed · October 2026

Quarterly panel, replaced at each course review. Each item comes with a date, a source and the type of claim: vendor survey, self-reported experience, forecast or study. The method in lessons 1 to 7 doesn't depend on any of them.

1. The manager as a lever

Microsoft, Work Trend Index 2025: the human manager of agents ("agent boss") and the metric balancing human oversight against agent efficiency ("human-agent ratio"). Work Trend Index 2026 (05/2026): 20,000 people in 10 countries; 16% are "Frontier Professionals," who use agents in multi-step workflows and redesign their work; when the manager uses AI in front of the team, perceived value goes up 17 points.

Type: research done by the company that sells the tool. An analysis in The Microsoft Cloud Blog (05/2026) reminds readers that these numbers shouldn't carry a business case on their own.

For the planUse it as a signal for block 2 (example and conversation). The proof is what happens on your team after the agreement.

2. The shape of the team

McKinsey ("agentic organization"): small teams built around outcomes, with 2 to 5 people supervising 50 to 100 agents. This is the consultancy's self-reported experience, not a measured study. Gartner (Oct 2024 forecast): by the end of 2026, 1 in 5 organizations will use AI to flatten their structure and cut more than half of middle management positions.

Critics point out that taking the layer off the org chart doesn't eliminate its work: coordinating, reviewing, training juniors.

For the planBlock 1 (roles) and block 3 (training) are the practical answer: who does the work the layer used to do.

3. Who is coming in

Stanford Digital Economy Lab (08/2026): a relative drop of about 19% in employment among young people aged 22 to 25 in the occupations most exposed to AI, without claiming that AI is the cause. Indeed: the share of entry-level postings fell from 29% to 10%.

The data shows a narrower door; it doesn't say what each company should do. The path in lesson 4 is one of the answers.

For the conversationCite the data as it is: what it shows and what it does not prove. It is the same care as in lesson 3.

4. HR and governance

Deloitte (Tech Trends 2026): onboarding of agents into data and operations, graduated autonomy, and training for human supervisors. Forrester (Predictions 2026, forecast): the large HR platforms will include "digital employee" management.

Okta (AI Agents at Work 2026, vendor survey): nearly two-thirds of organizations apply weaker security controls to agents than to employees.

Next review of this panelJanuary 2027, with the following quarter's overview. What is method (lessons 1 to 7) does not change; what is numbers and forecasts does.
